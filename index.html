POTOMAC CROSSING

Walk the National Mall from the Lincoln Memorial to the Capitol, loop the Tidal Basin, and ride the water taxi from Georgetown to the Wharf, from golden hour to the lit-up night.

Tip

You start on the Lincoln Memorial steps above the Reflecting Pool. Keys 1–9 fly to the viewpoints; F walks on from there.

The water taxi waits at the Georgetown landing: walk up, press E for the helm, or G to let the autopilot take it round Hains Point to the Wharf.

Press T to let the day run into night, when the monuments are floodlit and mirrored in the water.
